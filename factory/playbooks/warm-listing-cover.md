# Playbook: Warm listing cover that wins the tap
_Proven by: Grayson's standing bar since Oct 18-19, 2026: top-seller look, warm cozy palette; old dark/flat covers were rejected_

## Steps
1. Real product pages on a warm desk (cream/oat paper, wood, a mug or pen), never a flat title card.
2. Cover fills the thumbnail; title readable at phone size; no 'What's Inside' boxes.
3. Tablet or phone mockup when the product is used on a screen; hands holding it when printed.
4. Lens scores it; under 92 = reshoot tonight, not tomorrow.
