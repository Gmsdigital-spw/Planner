## The seven rules
1. **Research before making.** Every run, before a word is written, the Analyst pulls what is working right now in 3D cartoon story Shorts and writes `## Research` in the run notes: at least 5 Shorts from the last 7 days with views, length in seconds and what the first 1.5 seconds do. Free path first: the YouTube Data API through the Make `ytapi` lane (`GET /v3/search?part=snippet&type=video&videoDuration=short&order=viewCount&publishedAfter=<7 days ago>&q=<query>` then `GET /v3/videos?part=statistics,contentDetails&id=<ids>`; 0 vidIQ credits, about 100 YouTube quota units of a free 10,000 a day). vidIQ (`vidiq_outliers`, `vidiq_trending_videos`, `vidiq_similar_channels`, 5 credits each) only when `stats/channel.vidiqCredits` is 5 or more after the call; today it is 2 (renews Nov 19), so the free path is the path. Copy the structure (hook timing, length, story shape, series or one-off), never the content, characters or titles. Research is saved to `modules/youtube/intel/research` (shape in `stages/studio/references/db.md`).
2. **Hook in the first 1.5 seconds.** Line 1 moves (real motion from the free animation lane, `anim: true` on line 1, or a real clip) and the on-screen hook is a question or a shock: 3 to 8 uppercase words with a question mark, a number or a shock word. No slow intro: line 1 is 9 words or fewer.
3. **A real story that loops.** Setup, problem, twist, payoff, and an ending that cuts back into line 1 (loop true, last line reuses line 1's image prompt, pause 0.05). The twist line carries motion `punch` and is never the first or last line. Judge still scores hook, story, twist and logic 1 to 10 (every score 7+); the scorecard below is the second gate, not a replacement.
4. **Real animation, not a slideshow.** The twist line and line 1 get `anim: true` (the free Hugging Face lane; it worked on Oct 22: ep16 lines 5 and 7 got real motion). A third animated shot may go on the one emotional beat when the quota allows; never more than 3, never a paid tier, never a second account. Every other scene keeps the living-picture motion (push/pull/pan/orbit) and a dissolve. A build where the twist did not get real motion fails the gate and is rebuilt once; still no motion = not posted today, and the run notes say the lane failed.
5. **Natural voice, safe captions, a beat every 2 to 3 seconds.** Engine edge (andrew or ava) at 0.95 to 1.06; captions 3 words at a time inside the safe zone (the builder keeps the hook 12% from the top and the captions above 69% of the height; nothing else is added over the video). No scene longer than 4.0 s on screen (5.5 s with real motion) and a mean under 3.5 s: 9 words or fewer per line, 7 to 12 lines, 20 to 45 seconds total (aim 25 to 40). Length and beats are measured from the built file's word timings, not guessed.
6. **The quality gate.** `python3 studio/quality_gates.py short <id> --write` after every build (the GitHub Action runs it too and saves `yt/out/<id>.score.json`). 85 or more of 100 posts; under 85 names the checks to redo, Cutter fixes exactly those and rebuilds once (the day's one rebuild); still under 85 = not posted, the run notes say the score and the fixes, and the Writer's next episode starts from them. The ten checks and their points are in the code; a check the files cannot prove is FALSE. The vidIQ title check is 5 points so an empty credit balance can never block a 95.
7. **Learn every day.** Graph reads the last 7 posted Shorts every run (views, likes, comments at the same age; retention and watch time are not returned by the free API and stay "unknown"), writes the lesson and the one weakness into `intel/trends.lessons`, keeps a `kill` list in `intel/trends` of story shapes and hook styles that lost twice in a row (the Writer never reuses a killed shape without a written reason), and adds one notebook line per Short (`python3 factory/notebook/nb.py add --lane shorts ...`) with the tags that could explain its result (hook style, story shape, seconds, window, animated lines, score). Sundays: posting windows compared over the last 7; the window moves when the numbers say so (ep10's 12:49 PM CT is the one data point for a midday window; test it before trusting it).
