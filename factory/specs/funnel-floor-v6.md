# Funnel Floor V6 spec — the comment-the-word business (Instagram → DM → Gumroad) and Frontier Nav

_Standing instructions written with Grayson on Oct 20, 2026. Nothing on this floor runs until the two taps below are done; then a dedicated scheduled run ("Funnel run") is created with Grayson's go._

## The business
A Reel says "comment PLAN" (or the word of the week). Every comment gets an automatic DM with the link. The link sells the **GM Starter Kit** on Gumroad. Judged on four numbers only: comments, DMs sent, link taps, sales. No number is ever invented; "unknown" is written as unknown.

## Crew
- **Dispatch** (funnel_cmd) — runs the floor, writes the four numbers to modules/funnel/stats/<day>.
- **Scout-F** (funnel_scout) — finds the hooks that work in this niche from public Reels (vidIQ outlier search, 5 credits, Sundays only).
- **Hook** (funnel_hook) — writes the Reel scripts: 7 to 12 seconds, the first line names the result, the on-screen word is the call to action.
- **Reel** (funnel_build) — builds the Reel free (promo kit + factory motion), posts through the funnel account's own lane.
- **QC** (funnel_qc) — slop gate and 92 score, same as Herald's.
- **Ship** (funnel_ship) — keeps the DM text, the link and the Gumroad product in sync.
- **Stats** (funnel_stats) — reads comments/DMs/taps/sales each morning.

## The two taps (Grayson, in chat or in the app)
1. **The account.** A SEPARATE Instagram account for this business (never the shop's @gmsdigitalcreations, never the Gmula channel). Grayson creates it on his phone and names it; the crew never creates accounts. Then comment→DM automation: ManyChat's free plan (keyword comment triggers, DM reply, up to about 1,000 contacts) connected to that account, or a Make scenario if ManyChat is not wanted. Either is a new connection, so it is Grayson's tap; the crew only prepares the exact DM text and the keyword list.
2. **The product.** The GM Starter Kit on Gumroad: a bundle of the 3 best-selling or best-scoring planners (12+ pages each), one PDF with a cover page, $9.99, listed by Bolt through the normal gumroad_mirror op. Grayson approves the bundle list and the price first.

## Rules
- Free tools only (ManyChat free tier, Make free operations, the promo kit, the factory). Never pay for ads or boosts.
- The DM is one plain message: the link, one sentence on what the kit is, nothing else. Never follow, like or message anyone who did not comment.
- Reels: original characters and real product pages only; no brands, no music that is not generated by the music tool; same slop gate and 92 bar.
- Posting cadence once live: 1 Reel a day, posted by the Funnel run, measured the next morning.

## Frontier Nav (the "apps" lane)
- An original old-west style map app: parchment paper, ink roads, compass rose, plain-spoken directions. Built on free open data (OpenFreeMap tiles, OpenStreetMap search, OSRM demo routing) with MapLibre. No game art, no Rockstar or Apple assets, no names from any game.
- Lives at apps/frontier-nav/index.html in the repo → https://gmsdigital-spw.github.io/Planner/apps/frontier-nav/ . Version 0.1 is a working map + find-me + search + trail. Next: saved places, a "ride log", a share card. If it is ever sold, it is sold as its own thing (a PWA or a Gumroad download), never as a reskin of another product.
- Demo routing and search servers are for light personal use; before any public launch the crew proposes a free self-hosted or key-based option and Grayson decides.
