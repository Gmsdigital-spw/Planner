## Rules every marketing run follows
1. **Plan from the numbers.** Before picking what to post or pin, read sprint/main (flags), the newest stats/<day>, pins with a stats field, and social docs with metrics.availability "available". Prefer, in this order: "working" listings (push what sells), "looks, no sale" listings (new hook, different page as hero), the listing with the most views in 7 days, then anything not featured in the last 7 days. When pin stats show one layout/desk clearly ahead on outboundClicks or saves (2+ pins), lead with it and keep one pin per product trying something else.
2. **Holiday Mode share.** While Holiday Mode is ON, that share of the day's posts and of the night's pins aims at the target holiday (Christmas 60%: with one post per channel, 2 or 3 of the 4 channels feature a holiday product; with 2 to 3 pins, at least 2 are holiday). Inside 10 days of the holiday, stop featuring it and move to the next window or evergreen.
3. **Hooks (Flame).** First line names the buyer's result in under 8 words, the second line says exactly what is inside (real page list or count from the copy), the call to action is one plain sentence. No "elevate", "unlock", "game-changer", no emoji walls, no fake scarcity, no invented reviews.
4. **Lanes.** Say what the product is in the lane's own words: planner, wall art set, mug wrap, sticker sheet, editable invitation, party game pack. Mug wraps and stickers are shown flat on a warm desk, never as a fake 3D mug or fake hands.
5. **Separation.** The shop's channels (@gmsdigitalcreations, the GM Page, the SHOP YouTube channel, the TikTok account) never touch the Gmula YouTube channel, and the Funnel Floor's Instagram account (Phase 6) is a separate account with its own run. Nothing is cross-posted between businesses.
6. **Memory.** At the end of a run, append one dated line to the "What I've learned" section of factory/skills/herald/SKILL.md (Herald run) with what the numbers said and the one change for tomorrow; Pixel does the same in the Night Shift. Only inside factory/skills/.
7. **The Foreman's bar.** Posts per week = channels × 7; pins per week per the Factory goals; clicks to Etsy are the number that counts. The Foreman grades the wing on posts that went out, their scores, and clicks; never on how many ideas it had.
8a. **Never hold a day for Grayson's media (Oct 23, 2026).** When a post needs a video or photo he has not made, build the page-based version from the real PDF pages with social/tools (the look LESSONS.md proved) and post that if it passes 92; a channel that is disconnected gets one NEEDS YOU line (the one tap) and the other channels still post. Oct 22: 4 posts held for a video rebuild and 0 posted; that is the day this rule exists to prevent.
8. **Never.** Buy ads, boost posts, follow/like/comment/DM other accounts, create or connect an account, post anything that failed the slop gate, use copyrighted music or images, or put Grayson's real name or hometown anywhere.
