_Standing instructions written with Grayson on Oct 20, 2026. The Night Shift reads this file in STEP 0 and follows it as part of its prompt._

These rules sit on top of the Night Shift prompt. Where this file and an earlier step disagree about ideas, lanes or holidays, this file wins; everything else in the prompt stays exactly as it is.
- LANES. Every brief and product carries a lane: "planner" (planners, trackers, journals, budgets, meal plans, checklists, worksheets; this includes wedding planning binders, bridal and bachelorette planners, beauty, skincare and makeup trackers, nail and lash client books, hair care journals), "wallart" (the WALL ART LANE above), "mugwrap", "sticker", "invitation", "game" (party games, the existing game packs). Write brief.lane and product.kind with these exact words (existing "wallart" products keep "wallart"). The "text-based printables only" line does not apply to mugwrap, sticker or invitation; buildable for those means it can be made well with Pillow, reportlab, the kit fonts, the gs palettes and the wall_art.py backgrounds and motifs (bows, tulips, hearts, cherries, hydrangea, stars, stripes, gingham, leopard). Never draw characters, brands, logos, film or sports references, or trace another shop's design.
- IDEA ENGINE (replaces the "exactly 3 new briefs" count in step 2; the brief rules themselves are unchanged). Scout first writes 15 ideas in under 6 minutes of work, spread over the lanes (at least 1 per lane, at most 4 in any lane) and, while Holiday Mode is ON, at least 6 aimed at the target holiday. Each idea: {title, lane, buyer (one specific person), holiday (name or null), why (one plain sentence: the search phrase or gap you saw), verdict ("brief"|"hold"|"drop"), reason (one plain sentence for hold or drop)}. The Idea Judge gives verdicts with the auto-approver's own reject rules (repeat niche, brand or character, not buildable in its lane, window closing within 3 weeks, vague buyer, padded bundle, filler-word title, flooded niche with no angle): drop them; hold good ideas that lack a slot or whose window is not open yet; exactly 3 get "brief": the strongest, with one of them a wall art set as before and, while Holiday Mode is ON, at least one aimed at the target holiday. Write ideas/<day key> = {day, createdAt, holidayMode (bool), target (holiday name or null), items:[the 15], note (one sentence: what the market said tonight), source "Scout · Night Shift <day>"} (read first; if it exists, this is a second run today: add to items, keep 15 to 30). Then research and file the 3 "brief" ideas as full briefs exactly as step 2 says (evidence links, scores, total, summary), each with lane and holiday set, and set each idea's briefId. Log one event (crew "Scout", "Judged 15 ideas: 3 briefs, N held, N dropped").
- HOLIDAY MODE. Windows (days before the day): Halloween 45, Thanksgiving 40, Christmas 70, New Year 30, Valentine's Day 40, St. Patrick's Day 25, Easter 35, Mother's Day 35, Father's Day 30, 4th of July 30, Back to school (Aug 15) 40. Holiday Mode is ON when today is inside a window and more than 10 days before the day; the target is the open holiday with the biggest share (Christmas 60%, Thanksgiving, New Year, Valentine's, Mother's Day and Back to school 50%, Halloween, Easter, Father's Day and 4th of July 40%, St. Patrick's 30%). While ON, that share of the night's builds (rounded up, so with buildsPerRun 2 it is 1 or 2) aims at the target holiday; the auto-approver counts a holiday brief's timing as 5 when the day is more than 21 days out. FREEZE: inside 10 days of a holiday nothing new is built for it (the auto-approver rejects it: "window closes before the listing can rank"); briefs for it that are still proposed get status "rejected" with that reason. Christmas and holiday products: stripe page frames, the warm seasonal palettes (cranberry, evergreen, harvest, honey), and for covers and pins the kit's own drawn motifs (stars, bows, hearts, stripes, gingham) and words. Santa, trees, ornaments and other holiday figures appear ONLY as the kit's simple original shapes or as artwork whose licence you verified as public domain or CC0 on the source page (record {label, url, licence} in product.notes); no Pixabay-style "free" image without reading its licence, no clip art, no stock photos, no characters. Photos still obey HOUSE STYLE: a warm desk or a real-frame photo, never a drawn room.
- MUG WRAP SPEC (lane "mugwrap"; Forge, step 4). Build with Pillow at 300 DPI: 11 oz wrap 2550×1050 px (8.5×3.5 in) and 15 oz wrap 2700×1275 px (9×4.25 in), each as a flat PNG with a plain white or palette background (no transparency gaps) plus one PDF with both sizes at true size (reportlab, page size equal to the image). Design: typography-led (Lora and Poppins only), a short original phrase or a monogram-style layout, one or two kit motifs, colours from one warm gs palette; keep every word inside the middle 60% of the width so the handle and seam never cut a letter; leave 0.125 in clear at every edge. Write the real phrases first (no lorem, no brands, no song lyrics, no famous quotes). Product.file = the PDF; also upload both PNGs as assets and save product.extras = [{name, assetId, url, px}] so the Factory can hand them to the buyer. Pages = 2. Quill: use the Etsy category top sublimation-design sellers use in your research (record it in product.notes with a link); if you cannot verify one, use "Craft Supplies & Tools > Patterns & How To > Patterns & Blueprints"; materials "Digital download, PNG, PDF", and the description says exactly: sizes, DPI, that it is a sublimation/print file (no mug ships), personal and small-business use. Lens: photo 1 is the wrap laid flat on a warm desk with a pencil and sprig (mk.listing_cover with the two wraps as the "pages" if the kit accepts arbitrary images, else gs.cover_grid on warm paper); photo 2 shows both sizes with their dimensions printed; photos 3 to 5 as the Lens step allows. NEVER draw a 3D mug, a hand, a kitchen or a fake product photo; if a real mug mockup photo exists in the mockups collection (kind "mug", approved true) use it exactly as the wall art lane uses frames, otherwise stay flat and say so in the log. Price $2.99 to 4.99 unless research says otherwise.
- STICKER SHEET SPEC (lane "sticker"; Forge). One 8.5×11 sheet at 300 DPI (2550×3300 px) as PNG and as a one-page PDF, 12 to 30 stickers with a 0.08 in white offset border around each shape and at least 0.15 in between stickers, plus every sticker as its own transparent PNG (upload up to 25 as assets; product.extras lists them). Sticker types that fit the shop: planner labels (words like Pay day, Appointment, Water, Workout, Self care, Meal prep), habit and mood icons made of simple original shapes, holiday word stickers, checklist boxes, washi-style strips. Words are real and spelled right; shapes are original; no characters, no brand marks, no emoji glyphs. Product.file = the PDF (1 page) and the description tells the buyer: print on sticker paper, cut by hand or with a cutting machine (the PNG has a white border to cut along), what sizes the stickers are, how many, personal use. Lens: the sheet on a warm desk with a pencil and sprig as photo 1; a close-up of 6 stickers large enough to read as photo 3. Price $2.49 to 4.99.
- INVITATION SPEC (lane "invitation"; Forge). A 5×7 in invitation (two per US Letter page, with light cut marks) built with reportlab, with the design drawn once and then TYPE-IN fields over the changing lines (names, date, time, place, RSVP) using the reportlab AcroForm text field (d.c.acroForm.textfield(...) with borderStyle "underlined" or no border, the kit font, fontSize fixed, fieldFlags "", a sensible default value shown in the field); verify with pypdf that reader.get_fields() lists every field and that the page opens; also export a flat JPG preview (2000 px wide) with sample wording. One design per product, 2 colourways is fine (then 4 pages). Buyer-facing copy says: editable PDF, open in Adobe Acrobat Reader (free) on a computer, type your details, print at home or at a print shop on cardstock, 5x7 two-per-page, no physical item ships, fonts cannot be changed. Occasions that fit: wedding (invitation, save the date, RSVP card, details card as a set), bridal and baby showers, birthdays, Christmas and New Year's Eve parties, Thanksgiving dinner, graduation. Lens: the invitation on a warm desk with a pencil and sprig (photo 1), both colourways (photo 2), a close-up of the type-in lines (photo 3). Price $3.99 to 7.99; sets of 3 or 4 cards $7.99 to 12.99.
- GAMES (lane "game") and PLANNERS (lane "planner") are built exactly as before; add the lane word. Wedding and beauty planners: each page is real and specific (seating chart grids, vendor contact tables, 12-month countdown, budget tables with running totals; skincare AM/PM routine trackers, product shelf-life log, 30-day glow challenge), never a generic "notes" page.
- INSPECTION for the new lanes (step 7). The same 92 bar. The "file with 3+ pages" rule does not apply to mugwrap, sticker or invitation products: their page counts are what the spec says, and the other rules all hold. file /25 for mugwrap: pixel sizes exact, DPI metadata 300, words inside the safe zone, no transparent holes, PDF pages at true size; for sticker: border offsets present, nothing closer than 0.15 in, every single-sticker PNG transparent outside its shape; for invitation: every field present and typeable (pypdf get_fields), defaults sensible, nothing overlaps a field, cut marks light. Slop check adds: any fake 3D mug, hand, kitchen or room; emoji-style icons; filler phrases ("live laugh love" type sayings count as filler unless the brief names the buyer who searches it); a sticker sheet with repeated identical stickers to pad the count.
- GUMROAD. Bolt mirrors every passed product to Gumroad after it publishes on Etsy; you never touch Gumroad. Write nothing to stores/.
- Report line: add IDEAS counts from the ledger (15 judged, 3 briefed, N held, N dropped) and LANES (which lane each build was) to the IDEAS line of the report. ## SALES PUSH (Oct 21, 2026; Grayson's top order: get the shop's FIRST SALES before Christmas, then grow. This section is the newest standing order from Grayson and takes priority over older lines in this spec.)
- The facts tonight: 23 live listings, about 140 views total, 0 favorites, 0 sales. Views are low and nobody is favoriting yet, so the job is (1) products buyers already search for and (2) covers that win the click in a crowded Etsy search page.
- COPY BESTSELLERS, DON'T GUESS (Scout + Idea Judge). Until Dec 15, at least 2 of the night's 3 full briefs are Christmas or New Year products, and every brief must be modeled on proven demand: WebSearch Etsy for the niche and put at least one real bestselling Etsy listing (many reviews or a "Bestseller" badge) in brief.evidence, then say in brief.summary exactly how ours beats it (more items, clearer pages, better cover, lower price). An idea with no proof that buyers already pay for it gets "drop".
- BUNDLES (Forge + Quill). At least one build every two nights is a BIG bundle priced to the market (see PRICES; was $8 to $14): either one large pack (e.g. "60 Christmas Party Games", "Christmas Party Mega Bundle: games, awards, scavenger hunt, Secret Santa") or a combination of the shop's own existing products of the same theme rebuilt into one PDF. The cover shows the whole product with a big count ("60 GAMES", "120 PAGES"), the style Grayson likes. Bundle titles lead with the count and the main search phrase.
- COVERS THAT WIN THE CLICK (Lens + Atlas inspect). The Etsy search thumbnail is a small square: the product and its big count must read clearly at 300 px. Atlas scores a cover below 8/10 if the title or count is not readable at 300 px, and varies cover layouts (no two newest covers alike).
- SEARCH-FIRST COPY (Quill). The title starts with the exact phrase a buyer types (check it against Etsy autocomplete via WebSearch when possible), stays readable, and every tag is a real search phrase (no tags that only describe one page, like "cookie ballot"; no "digital download" tag, Etsy already knows).
- PRICES (Grayson, Oct 28, 2026: bundles at $8 to $14 sat against $4 to $5 rivals and lost price points every build): price against the page-one rivals Scout recorded for the same count and format, at or a little under their typical price. Ranges: $2.99 to $6.99 for single kits and planners, $4.99 to $9.99 for bundles. A price is "justified" when copy.priceNote names 2+ rival prices. Never change a live listing's price: the separate Pricing agent (scheduled task "Pricing agent (Ledger)") is the only crew member allowed to change live prices, and it runs its own rules.
- Next Move (10e) and the report: while sales are 0, the top recommendation is about getting the first sale (a Christmas bestseller-style product, a bundle, or a sale/discount Grayson can tap on in the Etsy app), never a nice-to-have.

## THANK-YOU PAGE (Oct 21, 2026, Grayson approved)
Every product PDF ends with ONE thank-you page (not counted in the page count) in the product's own palette and fonts, warm and friendly, with exactly this text:
- Heading: "You just made my day!"
- "Thank you for choosing a small, one-person shop over the big guys."
- "Quick favor? If this made your party or planning even a little easier, a 30-second review helps the next family find it. I read every single one."
- "How: Etsy app → You → Purchases → Leave a review"
- "Something not right? Message me first and I'll fix it fast."
- Footer: "With thanks, GM's Digital Creations"
Never offer anything in exchange for a review (no freebies, discounts or bonuses for reviewing): Etsy forbids it. Never guilt-trip or pressure. Mug wraps, sticker sheets and single-image files get the page as a separate last page of their PDF.
