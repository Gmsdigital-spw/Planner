# Etsy + promo pages V8 spec: the money lane (Grayson, Oct 24, 2026)

_Grayson's standing order for the shop GM's Digital Creations and its promo channels. Same authority as the other files in factory/specs/; where it differs from product-floor-v6.md, cover-styles.md or marketing-wing-v6.md, this file wins, except that cover-styles.md's Oct 24 section (Grayson's own Atlas covers: real pages on his scenes, one cream label card, no badge stickers, pills or emoji) stands and is restated here. Where we start, real numbers: 23 listings, 166 views in total, 0 favorites, 0 sales (stats Oct 22); 20 covers replaced on Oct 24 with the Atlas look; invitation briefs for birthday, baby shower and bridal shower filed Oct 24; commercials since Oct 22; $0 spent. Goal: first sales before Christmas. Every agent's job is making money._

## 1. Covers: top-seller look, every one different
- Photo 1 is REAL product pages filling the frame (60% or more), placed on one of Grayson's own scenes in `assets/atlas-pack` (the binder, clipboard, holiday table and oak frame: planner, clipboard, christmas and frame as `place.py` names them; the pack's phone scene is Ridgeline-only), plus one cream label card: the title in Lora, and the count big and readable in the facts line ("36 GAMES", "85 PAGES"). "Big count badge" in `studio/quality_gates.py` means exactly that: the count is big and readable on the card. Round badge stickers, benefit pills, emoji, 3D stickers, gradients and clip art stay retired (cover-styles.md, Oct 24).
- Realistic mockups only (hands, walls, tablets, desks from the pack), never a drawn room, a fake hand or an internet picture.
- Every cover different, checked by code before inspection: `python3 studio/quality_gates.py cover <cover.jpg> --recent <the other covers scored this run, under /tmp> listing-photos/auto/*/1.jpg listing-photos/atlas listing-photos/fun --checks <the five judged checks>` (pip install imagehash if it is missing; the gate keeps that order, newest first, and compares the first 20 covers it finds). Unique = a perceptual-hash distance of 18 or more against the last 20 covers. On Oct 24 the two closest of Grayson's own covers sit at distance 6 because they share a scene and a crop, so rotate the scene, the crop, the label position and the label colour every time; the same scene never goes on two products built back to back.
- Readable at 300 px (title and count), matched to the season and the buyer. `COVER_RUBRIC` is 100 points, pass 92; a cover that is not unique cannot pass whatever else it scores.

## 2. The 92 gate: rebuild, never skip the day
A product under 92 is made better and re-scored in the same run (07 Inspect's fix rounds: up to three), stays in stage "inspection" with the exact next fixes, and is finished FIRST on the next run before any new build. A night never ends with "nothing passed, try tomorrow" while a near-miss (80 to 91) sits in inspection: the Builder reworks it. Scores are honest; an 87 that fails beats a padded 92. Scrap only on the rules in 07 (retries 4, or market 11 or less after a real search).

## 3. Christmas products live by early November
Until 10 Christmas listings are live, at least ONE Christmas product passes every night (Holiday Mode share 60% stays; the SALES PUSH bundle rule stays: one big $8 to $14 bundle every two nights). Target: 10 Christmas listings live by Nov 7, 2026; the Report counts them every morning from the shop (listings whose title or tags say Christmas). Thanksgiving products stop being built on Nov 16 (10 days out).

## 5. Fix titles and tags on the best listings before adding weak ones
Grayson's V8 order: "Fix titles and tags on the best existing listings before adding more weak ones." This is his go for TITLE and TAG edits on existing listings only. Price, photos, description, category and deactivation stay on the "needs Grayson's go" list (shared/rules.md).
- Quill (Night Shift 05, after tonight's copy): for the 5 most-viewed live listings whose title does not start with the phrase buyers type or whose tags are not 13 real search phrases, write `listing_fixes/<listingId>` {listingId, title_before, title_after, tags_before, tags_after, why (one line with the number), status "ready", at} in the database. Never more than 5 ready at once; never a listing fixed in the last 14 days.
- Bolt (first run of the day, before publishing anything new): apply up to 2 ready fixes through the gateway (`{"op":"etsy","method":"PATCH","path":"/shops/<shop>/listings/<id>","body":{"title":..., "tags":[...]}}` per shared/gateway.md), GET the listing back to verify the title and tags landed, set status "applied" with the GET result, and add one line to products/<id>.notes {t, by:"Bolt", text:"title/tags fixed: <before> -> <after>"}. A refused PATCH is status "failed" with the error; Bolt never retries it the same day. New listings publish only after the day's ready fixes were applied.
- Judge each fix after 14 days by that listing's views and favorites against the 14 days before (the Night Shift Report, stage 10, writes this judgement from the stats docs); a fix that lost views is set to status "revert" and Bolt reverts it from the recorded before-values (status "reverted").

## 6. Promo videos are real commercials
Every promo video follows `COMMERCIAL_BLUEPRINT` (`python3 studio/quality_gates.py blueprint`): 0 to 1.5 s the finished product in hand or on screen plus a bold 4-word caption; 1.5 to 6 s the real Etsy listing scrolling on a phone; 6 to 12 s three real pages up close in quick cuts; 12 to 18 s the voiceover benefit (who it is for, why it saves time); 18 to 22 s the price or sale badge and "Link in bio". Rules: a cool natural voice (the free edge voices in the studio rotation), hype music low under the voice, captions that never cover the product or the phone, an animated background (the studio's moving palette or the Atlas scene with a slow push, never a flat card), and a new style every day (voice, opening, order and palette rotate; yesterday's combination is never repeated). The shop studio (`social/studio/README.md`) already builds this way; the blueprint is its checklist. The slop gate and the 92 score in Herald's 06 stay as they are.

## 7. Promo channels daily, judged in two weeks
Pinterest (Pixel makes 6 pins a day, Bolt schedules them), Instagram, Facebook and the shop YouTube (Herald, one post per connected channel a day) run on autopilot every day. TikTok is parked (Grayson's). On Nov 7, 2026 Hawk (inside the Herald run; this channel judgement is Hawk's one home, the 14-day listing-fix judgement in section 5 is the Night Shift Report's) judges every channel and every format by real numbers from Oct 24 on (views, saves, clicks to Etsy, visits): keep the winners, drop the losers (a channel or format with 0 clicks and under 50 views over 10 or more posts is dropped from the daily run and Grayson gets one line saying so). Nothing is dropped before Nov 7 on a feeling.

## Never
Spend money, run or boost ads, buy credits; publish past the daily or monthly cap; edit a live listing's price, photos, description or category, or deactivate one, without Grayson's go; nudge a score; use another shop's design, words or images; use internet pictures or stock people; claim a sale, review or result that is not in the sales log or Etsy's own numbers; skip a day because a product failed when a near-miss is waiting to be fixed.
