# Studio Floor V6 spec — the Shorts bar, two drafts, Spot commercials

_Standing instructions written with Grayson on Oct 20, 2026. The YouTube daily run (Vega's crew) reads this in STEP 0; the Herald run reads the Spot section on Sundays. Same authority as the run prompts, below them where they conflict._

## The bar Grayson set (his reference: a Pixar-style short where a strawberry walks a school hallway, blueberries in the background, real tears, a mouth that moves with the words)
1. **A real story.** First person or a narrator telling what a character did. Names, numbers, days, what someone said. A setup with stakes, two or three escalations, a twist that surprises AND makes sense looking back, a button line that cuts back into line 1. 11 to 14 lines, 38 to 52 seconds. Not a summary, not a list of fees.
2. **Two drafts, one winner.** Gags writes TWO different scripts every night (different premise, different emotional engine: one funny-twist, one heart). Judge scores both on hook, story, twist and logic (1 to 10 each, every score 7+). The higher average is built; the loser's premise goes to episodes/ as "unbuilt" so it is never repeated by accident. If neither clears 7 on every score, rewrite the better one once; still short = build nothing, say why.
3. **Emotion on screen.** Every emotional beat in the script gets its OWN line and its own close-up scene: "a single tear running down the cheek", "the hug, eyes closed", "walking away down the hallway, shoulders down". Give these lines the `orbit` motion and pause 0.4. Crowd scenes name the background cast ("other fruit kids at lockers in the background, blurred"). Never a scene with no character when the line is about a person.
4. **Motion.** The factory's living-picture motion is on for every scene (depth parallax, breathing idle, push/pull/pan/punch/orbit, dissolves). Use `punch` on the twist, `push` on tension, `orbit` on emotion, pans for wide shots. Consistent cast descriptions word for word every time.
5. **Voice.** Engine edge, voice andrew or ava, speed 1.03 normally; set the job's `speed` to 0.98 when the story is mostly heart. Write lines the way people talk; "..." where a narrator pauses. Captions 3 words at a time.
6. **Music.** `music` mood per story: emotional for heart, tense for drama/revenge, uplifting for underdog wins, cozy only for gentle family stories. Music volume 0.13 to 0.16, always ducked under the voice. Never a silent episode.
7. **Loop.** Line 1 is the ending as a confession or a question; the last line ends mid-thought ("...which is why"), pause 0.05, same image prompt as line 1, `loop: true`.

## Hero-shot lane (real animation: lip-sync, falling tears, walking) — OFF until Grayson says go
- What it is: ONE 4 to 8 second generated video clip per episode for the twist scene, made with vidIQ `vidiq_generate_video` seeded from the factory's own still of that scene (startFrameB64) so the character stays identical, then dropped into the job as that line's `clip`.
- Cost: duration × the model's per-second rate × 20 credits, quoted at submit (an 8 s veo-3.1-fast shot is on the order of 160 credits). The plan renews 150 credits a month. Buying credits or a bigger vidIQ plan is a paid change: it needs Grayson's go in chat, and the Foreman shows the lane as "off · needs credits" until then.
- Rules when ON: at most one shot per episode, never a regenerate of a delivered shot, never when the balance after the shot would be under 10 credits, always log the exact credits spent to pipeline/<id>.autoVideo.credits and stats/channel.vidiqCredits. Prompt stays family-friendly and uses the cast description word for word.

## Spot — commercials lane (Sundays, run by Herald on the shop's channels)
- One 15 to 20 second cartoon commercial a week for ONE GM product (the Marketing Wing's "working" or "looks, no sale" listing first). Structure: 3 or 4 skit lines with original characters (a tired party host, a mom with a budget binder, a teacher with a stack of games) that set up the problem in a funny way, then "the fix": the real product pages on the warm desk (Herald's promo kit) for the last 5 to 7 seconds with the caption "Instant download · link in bio".
- Build: Herald writes `yt/jobs/spot-<day key>.json` in the make_short format (id starting "spot-", 3 or 4 lines, cast, style copied from yt/jobs/ep12.json, voice ava or andrew, music "uplifting" or "cozy", loop false, end_text ""), pushes it, waits for `yt/out/spot-<day key>.mp4` (15 to 25 minutes, give up after 35), then appends its own 5 to 7 second pages tail with ffmpeg (same resolution, same audio format, music continued or crossfaded) and posts the result through the normal Herald lanes with the product link. The YouTube daily run ignores every job whose id does not start with "ep", so the two never collide.
- Never on the Gmula channel (@gmulaishere). Never a brand, character or celebrity. Same slop gate and 92 score as every Herald post. If the build fails or the Cloudflare key is rejected, post the normal pages video instead and say so in the log.
