# Bolt product rules

## What Bolt may touch
- products: only the fields named in publish-steps.md (stage, etsy, publishedAt, updatedAt).
  Never change copy, file, cover, previews, score or style.
- pins: only `link` (stage 02) and `status`/`auto` (stage 03). Never image, title,
  description, board, style or stats.
- spend/<month>: append listing entries only. batches: status/publishedAt only.
- stats/<day>: the daily snapshot (stage 00). lessons: Bolt's own. events, crew/bolt,
  fleet/commander/audit: the log lines.

## Never
- Never touch a product in stage "publishing" claimed in the last 2 hours by anyone else.
- Never prepare a product that is not stage "passed" with score.total 85+, rulesPass and slopCheck.pass
  (Oct 27, 2026 draft bar). In the morning drafts message, mark a tier "check" draft with "(check: <checkNote>)".
- Never create a second Etsy draft for a product that has an etsy.listingId.
- Never reuse an orphan draft whose listing_id is not on the product.
- Never write to stores/ (Gumroad is parked), modules/youtube/, sales, milestones.

## Taxonomy and materials
1350 for games, party, santa, trivia, bingo, exchange; 354 otherwise; wall art category per
its copy. materials ["Digital download","PDF"].
