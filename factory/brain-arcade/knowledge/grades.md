# Math skills by grade (what Domino may build for each grade)

Source: Common Core State Standards for Mathematics (corestandards.org), the standard most US
grade 2-5 teachers and many homeschool families follow. Every product names its grade and the
standard codes it practices; a problem above or below its grade is a QA fail.

## Grade 2
- Add and subtract within 20 fluently (2.OA.B.2); within 100 (2.NBT.B.5); within 1,000 with models (2.NBT.B.7)
- One- and two-step word problems within 100 (2.OA.A.1)
- Place value to 1,000: hundreds, tens, ones; skip-count by 5, 10, 100; compare 3-digit numbers (2.NBT.A.1-4)
- Even and odd; arrays up to 5 x 5 as repeated addition (2.OA.C.3-4)
- Time to the nearest 5 minutes, a.m./p.m. (2.MD.C.7); money: dollars, quarters, dimes, nickels, pennies (2.MD.C.8)
- Measure lengths; picture and bar graphs (2.MD.A, 2.MD.D.10); shapes, halves/thirds/fourths of shapes (2.G)
- NOT grade 2: multiplication facts, division, fraction numbers like 3/4 on a number line.

## Grade 3
- Multiply and divide within 100, all facts by end of year (3.OA.C.7); meaning of x and / (3.OA.A)
- Two-step word problems with all four operations (3.OA.D.8); patterns (3.OA.D.9)
- Round to nearest 10 or 100 (3.NBT.A.1); add/subtract within 1,000 (3.NBT.A.2); multiply 1-digit by multiples of 10 (3.NBT.A.3)
- Fractions: unit fractions, fractions on a number line, equivalent fractions, compare same numerator or denominator, whole numbers as fractions (3.NF.A.1-3; denominators 2, 3, 4, 6, 8)
- Time to the minute and elapsed time (3.MD.A.1); mass and volume (3.MD.A.2); scaled graphs (3.MD.B.3)
- Area (count squares, multiply side lengths) and perimeter (3.MD.C-D); quadrilaterals (3.G.A.1)

## Grade 4
- Multiplicative comparison, multi-step word problems, factors and multiples, primes to 100 (4.OA)
- Place value to 1,000,000; round any place; add/subtract multi-digit fluently (4.NBT.A, 4.NBT.B.4)
- Multiply up to 4-digit by 1-digit and 2-digit by 2-digit; divide up to 4-digit by 1-digit with remainders (4.NBT.B.5-6)
- Fractions: equivalent and compare (4.NF.A); add/subtract like denominators, mixed numbers; multiply a fraction by a whole number (4.NF.B); decimals to hundredths (4.NF.C)
- Unit conversions, area and perimeter formulas, line plots, angles and protractors (4.MD); lines, angles, symmetry (4.G)

## Grade 5
- Order of operations with parentheses, numerical patterns (5.OA)
- Place value to thousandths, powers of 10, read/write/compare/round decimals (5.NBT.A)
- Multi-digit multiplication fluently, division with 2-digit divisors, all four operations with decimals to hundredths (5.NBT.B)
- Add/subtract unlike denominators; multiply fractions; divide unit fractions by whole numbers and back (5.NF)
- Volume of rectangular prisms, unit conversions, line plots with fractions (5.MD); coordinate plane, classifying shapes (5.G)
