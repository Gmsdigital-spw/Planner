# Brain Arcade LESSONS (this lane only; numbers-only after Oct 25)

## Copied from the main crew on Oct 25, 2026 (copied, not moved; they apply to any Etsy shop)
- Covers: bold and colourful, REAL product pages are the biggest thing in the frame (40%+ of the Etsy square), ONE clean label card, title readable at 300 px. Never: gradients, round count badges, benefit pills, emoji, 3D stickers, clip art, cream-desk looks. (main LESSONS 10-21, 10-23; specs/cover-styles.md Oct 24)
- Variety: every cover must pass the side-by-side test against the lane's last 3 covers; a recolour of the same layout fails (main crew: 24 look-alike covers had 0 favorites on 188 views, Oct 23).
- Pictures (Grayson, Oct 25, 2026: "make sure they don't look like AI slop, use picture creating skills from other agents"): use the main crew's photo-scene tool assets/atlas-pack/place.py to put REAL pages on Grayson's real-looking scenes (clipboard for worksheets/games, holiday table for Christmas only), at most one scene photo per listing, plus real-page shots on bold colour. Never AI-generated pictures, drawn hands or rooms, clip art, emoji or badges.
- Exactly ONE cover per listing; replacing = upload new at rank 1 + delete the old one in the same run, then verify.
- Titles: the exact phrase a buyer types first, 140 characters max, readable, no keyword soup. Exactly 13 tags, each 20 characters max, no duplicates, real search phrases. Description 300+ characters, sells the result in the first two lines, lists what is inside, says it is a digital PDF download, ends with the exact AI line.
- Pass score 92 on the main rubric (file 25, copy 20, images 20, market 15, price 10, polish 10). An honest 87 that fails beats a padded 92. Up to 3 fix rounds a run.
- Anti-AI-slop approver: before scoring, put the cover next to the last 3 covers and fail on any line of the slop check (copied into stages/ba-night/references/rubric-ba.md).
- No build without market proof: 2+ opened Etsy item pages with 100+ ITEM reviews (search cards show the SHOP's count, not the item's) and a written gap.
- 2 new listings a day is a ceiling, never a target to beat; never ship under 92 to hit it.
- Honest reporting: real numbers only, "unknown" plus where it will come from; a green light means the RESULT is good, not that the task ran; a fixed problem is re-checked next run.
- A listing never goes live without ALL its photos and its PDF.

## Brain Arcade's own rules (Grayson, Oct 25, 2026)
- EVERY answer key is checked by tools/answer_check.py (independent math) before publish. A wrong answer is an automatic FAIL, whatever the score.
- Grades 2-5 only, each product aligned to skills actually taught at that grade (knowledge/grades.md).
- Previews show real game pages. Every listing carries the single-classroom license line and the AI line.
- Seasonal first: Thanksgiving live ASAP, winter/Christmas live by Nov 5.
- This crew posts ONLY to BrainArcadeGames (tools/shop_guard.py before every write). Never the same item in both shops.
- Free promotion only. No new social accounts until Grayson approves.
