# Atlas image pack (Grayson, Oct 23, 2026)

10 original AI-made backgrounds Grayson made for the crews. They are visual assets only:
never evidence of customers, reviews, sales or finished jobs. Originals stay untouched in
this folder; save edited versions elsewhere (listing-photos/, social/<day>/...).

Put real product pages on the blank paper, frames and phone with `place.py` (see its docstring):
`place.place("planner", ["page1.png", "page2.png"], "out.jpg")`.

## Etsy (landscape 1448x1086; place.py scene name in brackets)
| File | Scene | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| etsy/01_planner_mockup.png [planner] | Open gold-ring binder, 2 blank pages, oak desk, coffee | Planners, journals, trackers (2 real inside pages) |
| etsy/02_calendar_mockup.png [clipboard] | Clipboard leaning on a wall, olive branch, soft sun | Calendars, checklists, single-page printables, worksheets |
| etsy/03_christmas_games_mockup.png [christmas] | 2 sheets on a wood table, pine, cocoa, gingerbread | Christmas games and holiday printables ONLY (Nov to Dec 25) |
| etsy/04_framed_art_mockup.png [frame] | Oak frame with mat over a walnut console | Wall art and prints |

Rules for Etsy:
- Inside photos (2 to 5) and pins only, never the cover: covers follow specs/cover-styles.md.
  These scenes are the cozy wood-desk look Grayson retired for covers on Oct 21.
- Always with REAL pages from the product's PDF on the paper. A blank mockup never ships.
- At most ONE Atlas photo per listing, and never the same scene on two products built back to
  back. Same-template-everywhere is what killed the covers in October.
- Upscale with gs.save_jpeg to 2000x1600 like every other inside photo.
