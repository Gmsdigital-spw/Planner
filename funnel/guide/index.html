The idea

Digital products (printable planners, trackers, party games, wall art) never ship and never run out. So the whole business can run on software: AI researches what sells, builds the product, writes the listing, makes the photos, publishes it and promotes it. My job is to check the scoreboard on my phone.

What you need

  • A phone
  • A paid Claude plan (the AI that runs the crew)
  • A free Etsy shop and/or a free Gumroad store
  • Free accounts: GitHub (hosting) and Make (connects the apps)

The setup in 6 steps

1
Pick one product type. Printables are easiest: one PDF, sold forever. Start with something people search every season (holiday planners, budget trackers).
2
Build a control panel. Ask Claude to build you a private web app that shows your crew, your products and your numbers. Mine looks like a pixel-art factory.
3
Give every worker one job. A researcher finds what sells, a builder makes the PDF, a writer does the title and tags, a photographer makes mockups, a publisher lists it, a promoter posts pins and Reels.
4
Run them on a schedule. Claude scheduled tasks run the crew at night while you sleep. You wake up to a report.
5
Add a quality gate. Nothing goes live unless it scores 92/100 and looks like a top seller's listing. This stops "AI slop" from wrecking your shop.
6
Add a boss. One AI checks every worker each morning against real numbers (views, favorites, sales) and tells them what to fix. Mine is called the Foreman.

The honest part

This is not passive income on day one. New shops take weeks to get their first sale, and the AI plan costs money every month. I am building this in public so you can see what actually happens.
